# The Hollow Star

A cozy dark-fantasy game: a wizard's tower, an autumn village, a snowy wood, a town full of friends, and one quick quest down the Vesper Road.

Play it at https://ethan-goldstein.github.io/hollow-star/ — WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact, B to dance, ESC for options and to play with friends (host a world and share the four-letter code).

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT) and [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com) (MIT). Characters and props from [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.com) and [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) (CC0). The generated models, music and sounds were made for this game.
